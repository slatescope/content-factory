Privacy Policy – Slatescope

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Slatescope makes short explainer videos. Our internal apps (including Slatescope Insights Reader) connect only to Slatescope's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and other Slatescope accounts.

What we access

Performance numbers (views, plays, reach, retention) and public comments on Slatescope's own posts, so we can understand what our audience likes and improve our videos.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect data about anyone who has not interacted with Slatescope's own posts.
  • We do not sell, share or rent any data.
  • We do not use data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not post, reply, like or delete anything through these apps.

Storage

Numbers and comment text are stored privately for internal analysis only, and comment data is deleted when no longer needed.

Data deletion

To have any data related to you removed, email slatescope@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and we will delete it within 30 days.
